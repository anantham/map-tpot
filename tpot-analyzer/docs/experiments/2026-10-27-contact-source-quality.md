# An email mention is not necessarily a route to the account holder

27 October 2026. Offline source-quality experiment, completed against a frozen
private catalogue. This records findings, not an implemented admission policy.
No person-level examples, addresses or private research are published here.

## Question and falsifiers

H1, confidence 0.9: some platform/footer contacts were promoted too far, from
literal page mentions into per-account possible contact associations.

The useful falsifier is an explicit instruction connecting the route to the
creator, named project maintainer, organisation or purpose. Shared addresses,
organisation domains and footer placement alone do not establish irrelevance.
The reverse error matters too: suppressing a useful forwarding or support route.

## Population and method

The frozen catalogue contains 2,139 candidate rows across 1,008 accounts. Of these,
376 email rows cover 232 accounts, 297 literal addresses and 373 account/address
pairs. The rows retain 664 source references; 659 match admitted page snapshots
and exact email-field pointers. Five unsupported references belong to two
candidates, neither with another supported witness in this check. Unsupported
shape or current reader availability is not evidence that the content is false.

Select 50 email pairs for detailed review using exact-address recurrence and
source-purpose cues. This is a risk-targeted sample, not random sampling; the
other 323 pairs are counted but not classified. Inspect original source URL,
capture date, snapshot digest, pointer, anchor/context and retained HTML.
Independently audit proposed withholds and useful organisation controls.

A second sample covers 30 form/contact-page/website pairs: 11 forms, 12 contact
pages and seven website references whose notes suggest contact intent. Of its
39 references, 36 match current admitted pages; three remain unavailable to the
current reader. No destination or form submission is tested.

## Findings

| Detailed email pairs | Source-supported treatment |
| --- | --- |
| 3 | Shared publisher Press navigation, without creator-specific routing. A machine quality warning is justified for this association, not a global address block. |
| 35 | Named maintainer/creator references. Keep uncertain; obtain independent account binding before promoting or suppressing them. |
| 12 | Qualified organisation-purpose clues, including support, information, media and explicit forwarding. Keep purpose limits; do not relabel these as personal/general contact routes. |

The form/page sample has 19 plausible untested contact affordances, two
purpose-specific intake/email-link affordances, five no-verdict cases and four
ordinary project/site/community references rather than contact routes. An
explicit creator-directed form on a service host is a positive counterexample
to blocking all routes on that host.

## What failed

The first email classifier proposed 38 withholds using host branches after
describing its method as source-context review. Independent HTML inspection
reduced that to three: the other 35 sources explicitly identify a named
maintainer or creator. Their association to the listed account is unresolved,
not disproven. Preserve the initial packet and corrected dispositions.

The initial five-reference failure count lacked exact witness records. One
targeted replay recovered them: four email-field references lack a matching
admitted page; one text pointer is outside the supported email-field shape.
Do not turn these into five people, five false addresses or a proven policy refusal.

One helper called its output “human judgments.” These are agent-assisted source
assessments, not human curator decisions or gold labels. An earlier approval
question also used the preliminary 38-row count; its corrected scope is only
the three clear source-role cases and honest source-availability warnings.

## Integration consequence

Current candidate states are curator `possible`, `confirmed` and `rejected`.
There is no machine admission/quality state. The additive verifier deliberately
rejects predecessor removal or rewriting. Writing `rejected` on behalf of an
automated audit would fabricate a human decision; changing kind/scope/platform
would change the candidate ID and disconnect its history.

Proposed next slice: display a separate source-quality assessment, retain original
candidate identity and evidence, and place clear service-only possibilities in
a collapsible section. Human confirmations/rejections stay separate and visible.
Malformed/unavailable assessment data must not hide healthy candidates. Unknown
cases stay unknown; no host/address blocklist is inferred from this sample.

This proposal needs the operator's outstanding decision before implementation.
The audit changed no active catalogue, raw source, tag, curator event, graph,
post index or rollback copy. No paid fetching, outreach, restart or push occurred.

## Follow-up: explicit identifiers and a usable route delta

A saved-source follow-up found no account-to-person correspondence for the
original 35 associations. Twenty-three snapshots contained identical HTML
because they were repeated captures of the same shared Contact page, attached
to different research targets. They were not 23 different personal pages.

The source itself explicitly linked the named maintainer's X profile. An exact
saved-identifier sweep found one different admitted account, outside the original
21-account set but inside the authorized research population. This is a possible
publisher-claimed correspondence, not current identity verification. No identity
edge or contact route was assigned to it. The creator remained unresolved.

In parallel, admitted saved pages yielded 300 unrepresented contact-like pairs
across 176 accounts. Another 1,111 pairs had contact words only in surrounding
text, not a sufficiently specific anchor; these stayed held. The bounded final
delta has 46 possibilities on 34 existing cards: 27 untested forms, 18 contact
pages and one organisation-purpose email. No new person is counted.

Two negative acceptance results changed the preparation:

1. The low-level page iterator includes legacy projection bindings. Nine v1

## Uncovered candidates versus existing person-card evidence

proxy for missing evidence. Freeze the 1,650-account following cohort, its 48
explicit holds and the 1,008-card / 2,244-entry catalogue. Of the 1,602 non-held
accounts, 718 have candidates; audit the remaining 884 exact IDs, not names.

The maintained local readers return a profile locator for all 884 and at least
one other measured family for 522. The other 362 have only a locator in this
view. Overlapping family counts are 518 accounts with profile URLs, 12 with
literal bio email/channel mentions, 36 with preferred admitted pages, two with

Six also have source-qualified exported channel records, measured separately
from that overlap calculation. None of these counts confirms identity,
ownership, current availability, authorship or deliverability.

That lane also finds three literal bio-email occurrences across three accounts
