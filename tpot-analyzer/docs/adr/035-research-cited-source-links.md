# ADR035: Research-cited source links are not verified identities

18 October2026. Status:implementation of approved local consolidation.
Owner:Codex/root,canonical clean-main,codex/curator-review-shell. Extends ADR025.

## Decision

Project literal identity_binding.source_urls from explicitly selected retained work
cards as account→web_resource/research_cites_identity_source. The meaning is only
that saved research cited a page while associating this exact account with work.
Company/team/publication pages are allowed;they are not automatically owned profiles.
Do not create same_person,employment,verified platform identity or contact predicates.

Require an exact same-snapshot/card-pointer research_associates_with anchor with
matching numeric account,work title and URL. Retain the original research note,
retrieval caveat,source URLs/pointers and related work pointer. Keep source preparation
date literal and observation time unknown;it is not a URL-verification time. Ignore
private/unrelated draft,fit,outreach,availability and other fields. Whole raw sources
stay private and unchanged. No inferred URL/domain equivalence or name-based join.

## Consumer

Show a bounded separately-labelled disclosure in the existing exact-account Review
relationships. Add literal URL matches to the existing private POST lookup. Group
repeated URL/context values before presentation limits while retaining every cited
pointer;repetition is not corroboration. A shared URL never merges accounts. Source
notes are not interest-search/ranking text. Preserve old DTOs on absent imports.

Apply source policy to the work anchor on every read. A child cannot bypass a hidden
anchor. Missing projection is partial,not a complete zero;malformed citation evidence
must not erase healthy existing contacts/paths or lookup families. Safe links only;
no fetch/send/submit controls. Original raw/tags/old graph/package/index remain intact.
