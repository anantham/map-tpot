# Per-person saved evidence coverage

## Intent and scope, 22 October 2026

In Review, Browse saved following -> Load evidence coverage shows a dated,
read-only inventory for each exact numeric account. Local evidence sorting
composes the existing name/tag/clue filters and existing Review action. It does
not create a second roster, database, identity model or membership score.

The normal roster request stays local and unchanged. The explicit coverage
action may read the existing fixed public Archive publication metadata URLs.
No account, search term, tag, contact or private research is sent with that
check. Failure hides only that Archive channel; other evidence stays usable.

## What counts mean

- Platform names distinguish curator-confirmed, possible and rejected
  associations. These are not verified ownership or permission to contact.
  Explicit website publisher claims remain separately inspectable in the card.
- Directed stable-ID follows count distinct source/target pairs, not duplicate
  capture observations or a complete following list. Unresolved shadow links
  remain a separate channel and never become identity confirmations.
- Replies, reposts, quotes and mentions count distinct other accounts in each
  direction through the existing saved API post/reference reader. References
  do not establish endorsement, expertise, affiliation or membership.
- Saved API posts count distinct post IDs, including different retained text
  renderings only once. Their dates are as stored. Archive posts are counted
  separately only when the existing publication/checksum/eligibility gate passes;
  superseded, withdrawn, unavailable and not-indexed remain distinct.
- Pages use the existing source-qualified page reader. Work counts are saved
  associations, not verified authorship or current employment.
- Website-pass outcomes describe a bounded historical acquisition pass. No
  saved root, no retained page and privacy hold do not mean no contact route.
  Retained acquisition pages and connected/admitted pages are different measures.

No total combines overlapping API/Archive post counts, platforms or graph
channels into a confidence/completeness score. Unknowns sort after known values.
Open the existing exact-ID dossier for original passages, source dates and links.

## Source and preservation boundaries

`MAP_TPOT_FOLLOWING_COVERAGE` optionally points to the existing private pass
directory containing `cohort.json` and `coverage-ledger.json`. Its capture path,
exact-ID sets, bounded fields and dated outcomes must reconcile. Historical
rosters do not silently inherit current-capture outcomes. Privacy-held rows
withhold coverage details; malformed or missing coverage remains unavailable.

The existing candidate catalogue and separate curator decision journal are read
without initialization; decisions remain owner/account/candidate scoped. Reads
never alter tags, raw sources, candidate identities, journal or graph/index
bindings. Reloading coverage is explicit because the displayed inventory is a
snapshot, not a live atomic measurement of all sources.

Verification: `scripts/verify_following_review.py` exercises source-shaped
synthetic HTTP/UI cases with isolated persistence before application imports.
The existing combined Research Notes verifier remains required at handoff.
Real acceptance opens the saved cohort, sorts by a known channel, opens a person
and inspects original evidence, without fabricating a real confirmation or tag.
