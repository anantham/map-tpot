22 October 2026. Extends the approved ADR027 exact-account search journey,
without an index, source-policy, identity, tag or membership-model change.

## Operator control

In All saved evidence, use `dharma|Buddhism Marxism|Marxist` to require at least
one term from each whitespace-separated interest. Pipes have no surrounding
spaces and each alternative is one word. At most eight distinct words and the
existing120-character query limit apply. Ordinary queries retain AND matching.
No synonyms, beliefs, competence or group memberships are inferred.

Combine complete admitted per-word account sets: union within alternatives,
intersect across interests, then limit results. Different numeric accounts may
not supply each other's words, even with identical handles. Existing selected
network scope, one/two-hop paths and source refusal remain unchanged. Historical
handle alternative search is explicitly not searched, not a literal-AND zero;
its existing plain-word search remains available separately.

## Explanation and ordering

Show per-interest counts before overlap/limits and each result's supported
alternatives, with the existing original passages, dates, source links and refs.
Count each interest once in visible-bio and saved-text ordering, irrespective of
how many spellings matched. Preserve exact-identity priority. A same-post bonus
requires one alternative from every interest in one eligible post and cites only
the words actually present. It is not agreement, expertise or calibrated fit.

Hidden/missing sources stay partial or unavailable; independent saved evidence
remains usable. Optional synonyms do not authorize source-policy relaxation,
new acquisition, identity merges or fabricated judgments. Tests use disposable
state before imports. Acceptance includes cross-source branches, no cross-account
joining, invalid syntax, plain-query parity, source/scope refusal, complete pools,
group-aware ordering, exact post support, auth, UI mode guard/cancellation and
actual private result -> original evidence -> dossier/contact card.

and eligible independent code-only review. Preserve all raw sources, tags,
