# Saved contact and channel card

## Outcome and scope

In private Review, an exact existing account dossier can show saved contact and
channel clues near the top with their source details and coverage. This consumes
existing evidence; it is not another crawler or identity store.

Organisation inboxes, personal-email claims, page forms, channel-catalogue
mentions, website links and research citations retain distinct labels and
contexts. Equal literal values may be grouped visually, but observations are not
merged into one claim. Preserve source dates, instructions and unknowns. A form
may open its source page; the card must not send, submit, fetch or change tags.

## Assumptions and acceptance

- Existing exact-account dossier data and validated local sources should support
  a useful first card without schema changes.
- An account mismatch or unavailable source must not expose its clues; a failed
  projection must not hide healthy profile, work or post evidence.
- Catalogue mentions and page clues are not verified personal routes. Distinct
  instructions, pointers and dates survive grouping.
- Missing, partial, unavailable and truncated coverage remain distinct. An empty
  successful read does not prove that no other route exists.
- Raw/graph/tag data remains unchanged; source and synthetic checks plus Review
  inspection validate the journey.

## Deliberate limitations

The dossier starts from an X account ID. Native identities on other platforms and
cross-platform deduplication are separate decisions. A link is not verified
ownership, interest, belief, current reachability or delivery. Direct
profile-to-website link mentions may appear without cached page content, but are
still not owned profiles, reviewed routes or proof that the current person
controls the destination. Preserve separate observation pointers, snapshots and
dates when grouping identical URLs. The preview cap counts observations, not
unique URLs or people; explain grouping, truncation and hidden sources.

## Source boundaries

Apply the existing source policy before previewing evidence. Hidden Archive rows
do not suppress independently saved provider observations. A malformed or
unavailable link family must not hide healthy reviewed routes or catalogue
evidence. Do not expose raw snapshots or private filesystem paths. This card adds
no fetch, identity merge, tag mutation, mailto/action control or belief inference.
