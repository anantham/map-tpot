# Saved contact and channel card

Recorded 18 October 2026, 23:15 IST, during the first parallel prototypes.

## Outcome and scope

In private Review, opening an existing resolved account shows its saved contact
and channel clues near the top of the dossier, with source details and coverage.
This is a consumer of existing evidence, not another crawler or identity store.
Codex/root integrates in clean-main on `codex/curator-review-shell`; Luna agents
own the data projection, UI component and read-only contract audit separately.

Organisation inboxes, personal emails, page forms, channel catalogue mentions,
website links and research citations retain different labels and contexts. Equal
literal values may share a card, but their observations are not merged into one
claim. Source dates, instructions and unknowns stay visible. A form opens only
its source page; the card never sends, submits, fetches or changes tags.

## Assumptions, falsifiers and acceptance

- H1, confidence 0.9: existing exact-account dossier DTOs plus the validated local
  catalogue are sufficient for a useful first card, without schema changes.
- A real route-shaped synthetic fixture must appear through the HTTP dossier and
  rendered UI. Missing card/mount assertions must fail before integration.
- An account mismatch or unavailable cached page must not expose its clues.
  A failed new projection must not hide healthy work/profile/post evidence.
- Catalogue mentions and page clues must not become verified personal routes.
  Distinct instructions, source pointers and dates must survive grouping.
- Missing, partial, unavailable and truncated source coverage must remain distinct;
  a successful empty read must not claim that X is the only possible route.
- Focused/adjacent and combined verification, scoped lint/build, independent
  code/synthetic review, and actual private Review source inspection are required.
- Raw/graph/tag hashes and 93 existing tag assignments/events must stay unchanged.

Integration and review waits are separate from a 45–75 minute implementation and
verification forecast, starting 23:15 IST. Next checkpoint is the HTTP/mount RED
and source-shaped projection checks. If projection fails, retain the old dossier
and expose only the card's unavailable state; no new data acquisition is needed.

## Deliberate limitations

The current dossier still starts from an X account ID. Native Instagram-only
identities, cross-platform deduplication and follower acquisition remain later
architecture/acquisition decisions. A link is not verified ownership, interest,
belief, current reachability or delivery. The 138 qualification-rich citation
occurrences inventoried earlier remain deferred until this journey is delivered.

## Verified result, 23:27 IST

Served privately after assertion REDs, 62 initial focused backend/43 UI checks,
then a source-shaped initial-path-hop regression and final 632-backend/all-13
combined acceptance. Scoped lint/build and independent Gemini source/synthetic
review, including the substantive path correction, passed. Actual Review shows
7 values/11 observations for one saved account, with the organisation inbox's
original source pointer/date expanded and an explicit missing-cache state. At
319px viewport the card has scrollWidth=clientWidth=255px. Browser errors: none.

Protected graph/cache/Archive/tag hashes and 93 assignments/events are unchanged;
the active graph and rollback release were not switched. No paid fetching,
outreach, tag write, push or public deployment occurred. Opening the dossier uses
its existing current-Archive read path; the new card itself does no fetching.

Detached screen startup exited twice without readiness, despite a passing launcher
preflight. The same launcher succeeded in a managed foreground terminal (API
59435, UI 59496; health200, unauthenticated dossier401). This verifies the current
served flow, not detached lifetime across chat/app shutdown. Runtime durability is
a separate recorded dependency; no launch agent or broader exposure was added.

## Curator response cache boundary amendment, 27 October 2026

Every response from the Research Notes blueprint, including registered nested
routes, authentication refusals and validation errors, sets
`Cache-Control: private, no-store`. Status, body and curator authorization are
unchanged. The intent is to prevent private dossier/search responses from being
retained by browser or shared caches; this does not add identity, source or
publication-policy behavior.

## Saved encoded-page email amendment, 27 October 2026

The offline page extractor also recognises standard Cloudflare email-protection
links and marked data-cfemail attributes in the saved public HTML. It decodes
only a complete supported email representation, without executing JavaScript,
fetching protection endpoints or testing delivery. Malformed and explicitly
hidden markup does not become a contact clue; external CSS is not evaluated.

New derived pages use website-context-v3. Older v2 snapshots remain readable and
retained; reprojection preserves the original capture identity, gzip bytes and
date. Decoded clues carry a separate kind plus method, source attribute value,
element and line/column locator. Search, exact email lookup and cached-page
views expose that method and the existing original snapshot/field reference.
The graph still says page_lists_email, not person_owns_email or verified route.
Organisation or third-party recipients remain possible. No sending action,
identity confirmation, tag mutation, new store or network acquisition is added.
